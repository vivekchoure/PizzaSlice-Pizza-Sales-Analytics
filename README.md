# 🍕 PizzaSlice: Pizza Sales Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
**PizzaSlice** is a comprehensive SQL-based analysis project focused on understanding the sales performance of a pizza restaurant. Using **MySQL Workbench**, the project explores various aspects of the business such as revenue generation, product performance, customer preferences, and time-based sales trends. The objective is to derive meaningful insights that can guide strategic decision-making.

---

## 💻 Tools & Technologies Used
- **SQL**
- **MySQL Workbench**

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset replicates real-world sales data of a pizza restaurant and includes the following tables:

- **Orders** – Contains timestamps of customer orders.
- **Order Details** – Tracks each item ordered and its quantity.
- **Pizzas** – Includes pizza ID, size, and price.
- **Pizza Types** – Lists pizza names, categories, and ingredients.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Calculate total sales and revenue
- Identify best-selling and least-selling pizzas by quantity and revenue
- Analyze daily, weekly, and monthly sales trends
- Examine performance of different pizza categories (e.g., Classic, Veggie, Chicken)
- Discover peak sales hours and days to optimize staffing and inventory
- Provide strategic recommendations based on data-driven insights

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights
- 📈 **Revenue Trends**: Monthly and daily revenue patterns help forecast business performance.
- 🏆 **Top Performers**: Identified the most popular pizzas and profitable categories.
- 📉 **Underperformers**: Revealed the least sold pizzas that may need promotion or removal.
- 🕒 **Customer Behavior**: Determined peak order hours and days of high traffic.
- 💡 **Business Suggestions**: Recommendations for improving sales, staffing, and product mix.

---

## 📁 Deliverables
- All SQL queries structured and executed using MySQL Workbench
- Business insights and summaries based on query results
- (Optional) Screenshots or documentation for visuals and interpretation

---

## ✅ Outcome
**PizzaSlice** demonstrates how powerful insights can be derived from raw data using SQL alone. The project reflects strong skills in relational database analysis, business intelligence, and performance optimization in the food retail sector.
